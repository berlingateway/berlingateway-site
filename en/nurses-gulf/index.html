Global Career

The Roadmap

How to start while you are still working

Don't quit your job yet. Start the process silently from your current city.

  • 1

    Document Check

    Send us your CV and licenses. We verify if you qualify for the German "Anerkennung" (Recognition).

  • 2

    Language Preparation

    Start learning German at Goethe-Institut in Riyadh, Jeddah, or Dubai. You need B2 level.

    You can do this while working.
  • 3

    Job Matching

    We help you apply to German hospitals and employers. If you receive a job offer, you can sign the contract before leaving the Gulf.

  • 4

    Visa & Relocation

    We handle the bureaucracy in Germany. You apply for the visa at the German Embassy in your Gulf city.
