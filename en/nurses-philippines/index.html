Why Germany?

Why Choose Germany Over the Gulf?

If you're a Filipino nurse, you've likely considered the Gulf. But there's a fundamental difference: the Gulf offers a job contract. Germany offers a career path, with routes to permanent residence later under legal conditions.

The Triple Win program is a government-to-government partnership between the Philippine Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) and Germany's Federal Employment Agency (ZAV), supported by GIZ since 2013. Unlike private recruitment agencies, this program is designed for long-term integration, not seasonal labor. According to the Federal Employment Agency, placement, preparation and integration assistance are free for applicants, but applicants pay processing fees to the DMW (listed on the Federal Employment Agency page: DMW processing fee of USD 100, OWWA contribution of USD 25, PhilHealth contribution of PHP 1,200 and Home Development Mutual Fund contribution of PHP 100). Applicants also pay for a medical examination at a DOH-accredited clinic of their choice. Once you arrive, you work toward professional recognition (Anerkennung). Later residence options, including permanent residence, depend on your permit and the legal conditions.

The Timeline

The Realistic Timeline: From Manila to Your First Day

The programme does not publish a fixed total duration. The time depends on the application round, your German level, job matching and the recognition authority. Check the current announcement for the schedule of your round.

Practical tip: Steps 2 and 3 can overlap, saving you several months overall.

How It Works

The Triple Win Process: Step by Step

The Triple Win program is straightforward because it's government-managed. Here's what to expect:

  1. Register with DMW: Submit your application through the official DMW channels during open recruitment rounds.
  2. Language Training: Once accepted, you begin German language classes (A1 to B1) at no cost to you, as organised by the programme.
  3. Professional Preparation: GIZ provides orientation on the German healthcare system, professional standards, and cultural integration.
  4. Job Matching: ZAV matches you with German hospitals or elderly care facilities based on your preferences and qualifications.
  5. Visa & Deployment: After job interviews, you receive a formal job offer and work visa. GIZ assists with all pre-departure logistics.
  6. Integration & Recognition: Upon arrival, you work as an assistant nurse while completing your professional recognition. GIZ provides an integration advisor throughout your first year.

Life in Germany

Your First Year: Recognition and Salary

Upon arrival in Germany, you will work as an assistant nurse (Pflegehelfer) while completing your professional recognition (Anerkennung). How long recognition takes depends on the competent authority and your documents.

  • Pay: Pay is set by your employment contract or the collective agreement that applies to your employer, and depends on your role and on whether you are already recognised. Ask for the draft contract before you decide. For current figures, see the Federal Employment Agency's Entgeltatlas, which shows monthly gross medians of full-time employees subject to social insurance.
  • Integration Support: GIZ provides a dedicated integration advisor to help with administrative tasks, housing, language refinement, and settling in during your first year.
  • Family Reunification: Family reunification depends on your residence permit and on the family member. Ask the immigration authority before you plan.
Your Educational Foundation

How Your Nursing Education Is Assessed

German recognition authorities assess your nursing education and professional experience individually, based on your documents. There is no automatic equivalence. Keep your diploma, transcript, PRC registration and employment certificates ready.
