Process

How a Referral Works — From First Contact to Discharge

Every referral follows a structured pathway. Nothing moves forward without your explicit approval at each stage.

1
Send the Medical File or Referral Letter
By email or secure transfer — reports, imaging, prior surgical notes. The more complete the file, the more precise our assessment. We acknowledge receipt within 24 hours.
2
We Identify the Right Department and Specialist
We review the case and liaise with the appropriate department at a German university hospital. We return to you with the hospital name, department, estimated cost, and proposed timeline — before any commitment.
3
Hospital Issues Official Invitation and Cost Estimate
All clinical documentation, official invitations, and cost estimates are issued directly by the treating institution. This document serves as the basis for the patient's medical visa application.
4
We Coordinate Arrival, Accommodation & Accompaniment
Our Arabic-speaking coordinator meets the patient at Berlin airport and accompanies them to every appointment — translating, documenting, and ensuring nothing is lost between patient and physician.
5
Full Medical Report Returned Upon Request
After treatment or discharge, the full clinical report is issued by the hospital or professor. We forward this to the referring physician or institution upon request, and coordinate any follow-up care in the patient's home country.
