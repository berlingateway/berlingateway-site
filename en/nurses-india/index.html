Government programme

Triple Win Programme — Kerala & Telangana

A government-to-government recruitment channel run by Germany's Federal Employment Agency (ZAV) and GIZ, in partnership with each state's own agency.

Qualified Nurses

Triple Win — Registered Nurses

For nurses who already hold a recognised nursing qualification (GNM, B.Sc Nursing, or equivalent) and are ready to work. Kerala candidates apply through NORKA Roots; Telangana candidates apply through TOMCOM.

Nursing Trainee / Ausbildung

Triple Win — Trainee Programme

A separate track for candidates without a nursing qualification yet, who complete a 3-year vocational nursing training (Ausbildung) in Germany. Eligibility and entry requirements differ from the qualified-nurse track.

How to Apply

Applications are submitted directly through the official state agency for your region:

  • Kerala: NORKA Roots — norkaroots.org
  • Telangana: TOMCOM — tomcom.telangana.gov.in

Eligibility criteria — age, professional experience, and required German level — vary between application rounds and are set by NORKA Roots, TOMCOM, and the Federal Employment Agency. Always check the current call for applications on the official website before preparing your documents.

What "No Placement Fees" Means

The Triple Win programme itself charges no placement or recruitment fees. However, personal costs may still apply depending on the intake — for example, document translation, notarisation, travel to interviews or exams, and in some rounds, part of the language course.

General Process

  1. Official application through NORKA Roots or TOMCOM
  2. Eligibility screening and interview
  3. German language preparation (up to B1, sometimes B2 depending on the round)
  4. Employer matching with a German hospital or care facility
  5. Recognition and visa coordination
  6. Arrival and integration support in Germany
Berlin Gateway does not administer the Triple Win programme and cannot guarantee acceptance. We help explain how the pathway works and support candidates who are not eligible for it in exploring the individual recognition route below.

All other Indian states

Individual Recognition Route

For nurses from states outside the Triple Win partnership, or those who prefer to start the process independently.

Nursing Is a Regulated Profession

In Germany, you cannot practise or use the professional title Pflegefachperson without formal recognition of your qualification and the required professional licence (Berufserlaubnis). This applies regardless of your experience or the state you apply from.

Who Decides — and Where to Apply

Your application is submitted to the competent recognition authority in the German federal state where you intend to work — not to ZAB. You can identify the correct authority using the official Anerkennungs-Finder. Once granted, recognition is valid throughout Germany.

No Employer Required to Begin

You can start the recognition process from India, before securing a job offer. An employer becomes relevant later — for the actual job placement and, eventually, the work visa — but is not a requirement to submit your recognition application.

How Indian Qualifications Are Assessed

B.Sc Nursing, GNM, and ANM are evaluated individually — there is no automatic equivalence. The recognition authority reviews the duration and content of your training, theoretical and practical hours, and your professional experience against the German reference qualification (Pflegefachfrau/Pflegefachmann). ANM in particular is not treated as automatically equivalent to Pflegefachperson.

Documents Typically Requested

  • Diploma/degree certificate and academic transcript
  • Description of subjects, and theoretical/practical hours completed
  • Indian nursing council registration
  • Proof of professional experience, where applicable
  • Identity documents and CV
  • Certified translations, as required by the competent authority

Exact requirements vary by state and by case — the list above is indicative, not exhaustive.

Possible Outcomes

  • Full recognition
  • Recognition with identified differences (Defizitbescheid)
  • Anpassungslehrgang (supervised adaptation period)
  • Kenntnisprüfung (knowledge/skills examination)
  • In some cases, refusal or a request for additional documentation

Language and Other Requirements

Full professional practice generally requires German at B2 level, alongside proof of health fitness and professional reliability (Zuverlässigkeit). These are assessed separately from your qualification equivalence.

Step by step

From Application to Arrival

The overall journey follows a similar shape for both pathways, though timing and sequence differ in the details.

  • 1

    Choose Your Pathway

    Confirm whether you're eligible for Triple Win (Kerala/Telangana) or need the individual recognition route.

  • 2

    Submit Application or Recognition Request

    Apply through NORKA Roots/TOMCOM, or submit your file to the competent state authority via the Anerkennungs-Finder.

  • 3

    Language Preparation

    Work towards German B1/B2, depending on your pathway and the requirements of your target state or programme round.

  • 4

    Recognition Decision

    Receive your outcome — full recognition, or a notice specifying an Anpassungslehrgang or Kenntnisprüfung.

  • 5

    Employer & Visa

    Secure a job offer (via Triple Win matching or independently) and apply for your work visa.

  • 6

    Arrival & Integration

    Relocate to Germany, complete any remaining compensatory measures, and begin practising as a Pflegefachperson.

Timelines vary significantly by pathway, state, and individual case — this overview shows the general sequence, not a fixed schedule.
