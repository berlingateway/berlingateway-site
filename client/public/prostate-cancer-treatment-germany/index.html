About the Condition

Prostate Cancer Management in Germany

Prostate cancer management in Germany is conducted within certified prostate cancer centers that apply multidisciplinary tumor board review for all cases. German university urology departments offer the full spectrum of treatment options — from active surveillance for low-risk disease to radical prostatectomy, radiation therapy, and systemic therapies for advanced and metastatic disease.

Risk stratification based on PSA level, Gleason score, and clinical stage guides treatment decisions. PSMA PET-CT has become the standard staging tool in Germany for detecting nodal and distant metastases with higher sensitivity than conventional imaging, particularly in the context of biochemical recurrence.

Why Germany

Why Treatment in Germany

German university urology centers perform robotic-assisted radical prostatectomy (RARP) with high surgical volume, which is associated with favourable functional and oncological outcomes. PSMA-targeted radioligand therapy is available at German nuclear medicine centers for eligible patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer, and focal therapy options including HIFU are offered at selected centers.

Specialist evaluation involves a review of PSA history, biopsy pathology, staging imaging including PSMA PET-CT where available, and prior treatment history. German urological oncologists assess whether the current risk stratification is accurate, determine the most appropriate primary or salvage treatment approach, and evaluate eligibility for systemic or radioligand therapies.

Treatment Pathways

Treatment Options by Stage

Treatment selection in German prostate cancer centers is determined by a multidisciplinary tumor board — urologist, radiation oncologist, and medical oncologist reviewing each case together. The approach depends on PSA level, Gleason score, clinical stage, and patient preference.

Low to Intermediate Risk

Localised Disease

  • Active surveillance (PSA monitoring)
  • Robotic radical prostatectomy (da Vinci)
  • Stereotactic body radiotherapy (SBRT)
  • Brachytherapy (seed implant)
  • Focal HIFU therapy
High Risk / Locally Advanced

Advanced Local Disease

  • Radical prostatectomy + lymph node dissection
  • External beam radiotherapy + ADT
  • Extended pelvic lymph node dissection
  • Salvage radiation after PSA recurrence
Metastatic / Stage IV

Advanced & Metastatic

  • PSMA radioligand therapy (Lu-177)
  • Enzalutamide / Abiraterone
  • Docetaxel / Cabazitaxel chemotherapy
  • PARP inhibitors (BRCA-mutated)
  • Liver metastases: TACE / RFA / SBRT
Advanced Diagnostics

PSMA PET-CT — Germany's Diagnostic Advantage

PSMA PET-CT has transformed prostate cancer staging in Germany. It detects nodal and distant metastases — including liver metastases — with significantly higher sensitivity than conventional CT or bone scan, particularly in cases of PSA recurrence after local treatment.

Advanced Disease

Prostate Cancer with Liver Metastases — Treatment in Germany

Liver metastases from prostate cancer (visceral metastatic disease) represent an advanced stage requiring a coordinated multidisciplinary approach. German university centers manage this with concurrent urological oncology, interventional radiology, and hepatobiliary surgery involvement.

Local Treatment Options for Liver Metastases

TACE

Transarterial chemoembolisation — delivers chemotherapy directly to liver lesions via catheter

SBRT / SABR

Stereotactic body radiotherapy — highly precise radiation targeting individual liver metastases

RFA / MWA

Radiofrequency or microwave ablation — minimally invasive destruction of liver lesions under imaging

SIRT / Y-90

Selective internal radiation — microspheres delivering internal radiation to liver metastases

Systemic treatment — typically Lu-177 PSMA therapy, novel hormonal agents, or chemotherapy — runs in parallel with local liver-directed treatment where appropriate. All cases are reviewed in the weekly tumor board before any treatment decision is made.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions — Prostate Cancer Treatment in Germany

What are the treatment options for prostate cancer in Germany?
German university centers offer the full spectrum: active surveillance, robotic radical prostatectomy (da Vinci), SBRT, brachytherapy, HIFU, PSMA radioligand therapy (Lu-177), novel hormonal agents (Enzalutamide, Abiraterone), and chemotherapy. Treatment is selected by a multidisciplinary tumor board after reviewing your PSA, Gleason score, staging, and imaging.
What is PSMA PET-CT and is it available in Germany?
PSMA PET-CT uses a prostate-specific membrane antigen tracer to detect nodal and distant metastases with far greater sensitivity than conventional CT or bone scan — particularly at low PSA values during biochemical recurrence. It is standard practice at German university centers and available for international patients.
How does robotic prostatectomy in Germany compare to other countries?
German university urology departments perform high volumes of robotic-assisted radical prostatectomy (RARP) using the da Vinci Xi system. High surgical volume is consistently associated with better functional outcomes (continence, potency preservation) and oncological results. All cases undergo preoperative multidisciplinary review.
What documents are needed for a prostate cancer specialist evaluation in Germany?
Submit: PSA history (all values with dates), biopsy pathology report with Gleason score and number of cores positive, staging imaging (CT chest/abdomen/pelvis, bone scan, or PSMA PET-CT if available), operative reports if prior treatment has been performed, and current medication list including any hormonal therapy.
Can international patients access PSMA radioligand therapy in Germany?
Yes. Lu-177 PSMA therapy is available at German university nuclear medicine centers for eligible patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) who are PSMA-positive on PET-CT. Eligibility is confirmed after specialist review. We coordinate access and scheduling for international patients.
Is prostate cancer with liver metastases treatable in Germany?
Yes. Visceral metastatic prostate cancer, including liver metastases, is managed in German university centers using a combination of systemic therapy (Lu-177 PSMA, novel hormonal agents, or chemotherapy) and liver-directed local treatments such as TACE, SBRT, or radiofrequency ablation. All cases are reviewed by a multidisciplinary tumor board.
What is the cost of prostate cancer treatment in Germany?
Costs are determined case by case depending on stage, treatment modality, and hospital. There is no standard fixed price. We obtain a formal cost estimate from the hospital at no charge — submit your medical file and we will return with a detailed breakdown within 3–5 working days.
Can I get a second opinion on my prostate cancer diagnosis from a German specialist?
Yes. We coordinate second opinion consultations with department heads at Charité, Vivantes, and other certified centers. Submit your pathology report, PSA history, biopsy results, and imaging — the specialist reviews remotely and provides a written assessment, typically within 5–7 working days.
