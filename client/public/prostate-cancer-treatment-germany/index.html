About the Condition

Prostate Cancer Management in Germany

Prostate cancer management in Germany is conducted within certified prostate cancer centers that apply multidisciplinary tumor board review for all cases. German university urology departments offer the full spectrum of treatment options — from active surveillance for low-risk disease to radical prostatectomy, radiation therapy, and systemic therapies for advanced and metastatic disease.

Risk stratification based on PSA level, Gleason score, and clinical stage guides treatment decisions. PSMA PET-CT has become the standard staging tool in Germany for detecting nodal and distant metastases with higher sensitivity than conventional imaging, particularly in the context of biochemical recurrence.

Why Germany

Why Treatment in Germany

German university urology centers perform robotic-assisted radical prostatectomy (RARP) with high surgical volume, associated with favourable functional and oncological outcomes. PSMA-targeted radioligand therapy is available at German nuclear medicine centers for eligible patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer, and focal therapy options including HIFU are offered at selected centers.

Specialist evaluation involves a review of PSA history, biopsy pathology, staging imaging including PSMA PET-CT, and prior treatment history. German urological oncologists assess whether the current risk stratification is accurate, determine the most appropriate primary or salvage treatment approach, and evaluate eligibility for systemic or radioligand therapies.

Treatment Pathways

Treatment Options by Stage

Treatment selection in German prostate cancer centers is determined by a multidisciplinary tumor board — urologist, radiation oncologist, and medical oncologist reviewing each case together. The approach depends on PSA level, Gleason score, clinical stage, and patient preference.

Low to Intermediate Risk

Localised Disease

  • Active surveillance (PSA monitoring)
  • Robotic radical prostatectomy (da Vinci)
  • Stereotactic body radiotherapy (SBRT)
  • Brachytherapy (seed implant)
  • Focal HIFU therapy
High Risk / Locally Advanced

Advanced Local Disease

  • Radical prostatectomy + lymph node dissection
  • External beam radiotherapy + ADT
  • Extended pelvic lymph node dissection
  • Salvage radiation after PSA recurrence
Metastatic / Stage IV

Advanced & Metastatic

  • PSMA radioligand therapy (Lu-177)
  • Enzalutamide / Abiraterone
  • Docetaxel / Cabazitaxel chemotherapy
  • PARP inhibitors (BRCA-mutated)
  • Liver metastases: TACE / RFA / SBRT

Advanced Diagnostics

PSMA PET-CT — Germany's Diagnostic Advantage

PSMA PET-CT has transformed prostate cancer staging in Germany. It detects nodal and distant metastases — including liver metastases — with significantly higher sensitivity than conventional CT or bone scan, particularly in cases of PSA recurrence after local treatment.

Ga-68
Gallium PSMA PET-CT
Standard PSMA imaging — detects recurrence at PSA levels as low as 0.2 ng/mL
Lu-177
PSMA Radioligand Therapy
Theranostic approach for mCRPC — available after VISION trial results
mpMRI
Multiparametric MRI
PI-RADS scoring for local staging and targeted biopsy guidance — standard in German centers
NGS
Molecular Tumour Profiling
BRCA1/2 and HRR gene panel testing — determines eligibility for PARP inhibitors and clinical trials

Advanced Disease

Prostate Cancer with Liver Metastases — Treatment in Germany

Liver metastases from prostate cancer (visceral metastatic disease) represent an advanced stage requiring a coordinated multidisciplinary approach. German university centers manage this with concurrent urological oncology, interventional radiology, and hepatobiliary surgery involvement.

TACE

Transarterial Chemoembolisation

Delivers chemotherapy directly to liver lesions via catheter

SBRT / SABR

Stereotactic Radiotherapy

Highly precise radiation targeting individual liver metastases

RFA / MWA

Thermal Ablation

Radiofrequency or microwave ablation — minimally invasive destruction of liver lesions under imaging
