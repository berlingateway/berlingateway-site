Who the assessment is for

For international professionals considering Germany

The assessment is a starting point for people with qualifications or work experience from outside Germany who want to understand which questions and possible routes are relevant to their situation. Berlin Gateway’s guidance covers health and care profiles such as nursing, physiotherapy and medicine, as well as other professional fields including IT, engineering and skilled trades.

The relevant route depends on your profession, qualifications, experience, language level and current circumstances. An initial profile assessment is not an eligibility decision by an authority or employer.

Possible pathways

Different profiles can raise different questions

These are possible topics for an initial assessment, not a promise that a particular route is available or suitable for every person.

Recognition of professional qualifications

For regulated professions, formal recognition or a professional licence may be relevant. The competent authority assesses the qualification and applicable requirements for the individual profession and case.

Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)

This may be relevant to eligible skilled professionals who want to explore job opportunities without already having a job offer. Its criteria and residence conditions must be checked for the individual case.

Skilled-worker or work visa

A work-related residence route may be relevant where there is a suitable employment offer and the applicable qualification and other conditions are met. Requirements differ by route and profession.

Vocational training (Ausbildung)

Company-based dual training and school-based training are different forms. The training place, programme, documents, language and residence questions may differ between them.

Direct employment

For some profiles, employment with a German employer may be a relevant goal. The employer decides whether to hire, and any residence route is assessed separately.

Documents to prepare

Have relevant records available

The assessment form asks for a CV. Depending on your profession and the route you are exploring, other supporting records may also be useful. Examples include:

  • A current CV with your education and work history.
  • Your diploma or degree certificate and academic transcript.
  • A description of subjects studied and theoretical or practical hours, where relevant to the profession.
  • Evidence of professional experience and, for regulated professions, registration or a licence where applicable.
  • Language certificates, identity documents and translations where relevant to your route or requested by the competent authority.

This is an indicative list, not a universal checklist. Requirements vary by profession, route, provider and competent authority. The assessment itself does not replace any official document or recognition process.

Privacy and consent

Your information is used for the initial profile assessment

Berlin Gateway processes the information and documents you submit for the initial assessment of your professional profile and possible next steps. Read the English privacy policy for further information. Your profile or documents are shared with a specific potential employer only after you have given separate prior written consent to that disclosure.

Important limitation

An assessment is not a guaranteed outcome

Berlin Gateway does not guarantee professional recognition, a job or employment contract, a visa, or entry to Germany. Competent authorities decide on recognition and residence matters. Employers decide whether to hire.
