Is the career path in Germany right for you?

This page is for qualified physiotherapists trained abroad who want to understand the professional steps before applying for a job in Germany.

Professional qualification

You need a completed degree or qualification in physiotherapy from your country of origin, with educational and professional documents you can provide when needed.

Recognition readiness

Working under the professional title in Germany involves recognition and licensing procedures with the competent authority.

German language

Language requirements may differ depending on your profile and the competent authority. The current opportunity in Berlin requires at least B2.

Relocation and work

The path suits those who are ready to move to Germany and to work and live in Berlin, where qualification and opportunity match.

Recognition of qualifications

Physiotherapy is a regulated profession in Germany

Working under the professional title “physiotherapist” is linked to the relevant recognition and licensing procedures. The competent authority looks at the qualification, the documents and other requirements depending on the case and jurisdiction; the path required therefore differs from one profile to another.

Start by understanding your professional situation

Before planning a move, gather your qualification documents and find out which recognition path suits your case. The official Service Berlin provides information on the procedure in Berlin.

How we work with you

  1. Assessment: You send us your documents. We check completeness and how your profile fits the German system.
  2. Recognition: We review the documents you already have and show what is missing from your file and what the next step is, depending on your situation.
  3. Employers: We coordinate with suitable employers. We send your profile to them only after you have agreed to how it is presented and which details it contains.
  4. Employment contract and entry procedures: When there is a concrete job offer, we explain the procedural steps required.

Decisions on recognition, residence and visa are made exclusively by the competent authorities.

Documents we need (PDF)

  • CV with exact dates
  • Bachelor's degree or diploma and transcript
  • Overview of subjects and study hours
  • Internship certificates and work references
  • German language certificate with issuer and date, if available
  • Professional registration or licence to practise, if available
  • Recognition decision, if available
  • Passport

You can also send everything as one clearly readable PDF. The file can be submitted for the first assessment without a language certificate, but applying for the current opportunity in Berlin requires at least B2.

FAQ

Do I need German? The current opportunity in Berlin requires at least B2. Language requirements for recognition differ depending on the competent authority and your case.

How long does the process take? It depends on the authority, the federal state and how complete your documents are. We do not state a fixed duration.

What does it cost? Costs vary depending on the recognition path, the authority and any courses or exams required. We explain the known costs and the scope of our services before you agree to continue.

Do you guarantee me a job? We do not guarantee a job. The employer decides on hiring; the competent authorities decide on recognition and visa.
