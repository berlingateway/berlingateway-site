Why Germany?

Why Choose Germany Over the Gulf?

If you're an Indonesian nurse, you've likely considered the Middle East. But there's a fundamental difference: the Gulf offers a job contract. Germany offers a career path and permanent residency.

The Triple Win program is a government-to-government partnership between Indonesia's Migrant Workers Protection Agency (BP2MI) and Germany's Federal Employment Agency (ZAV), supported by GIZ since 2021. Unlike private recruitment agencies, this program is designed for long-term integration, not seasonal labor. You pay nothing. German employers cover all placement and language training costs. Once you arrive, you work toward professional recognition (Anerkennung) and a clear path to permanent residency and citizenship.

The Timeline

The Realistic Timeline: From Jakarta to Your First Day

The complete process, from initial application to professional recognition, typically takes between 12 and 14 months.

Phase Approximate Duration What Happens
1. BP2MI Application & Selection 2–3 months Submit application through SISKOP2MI portal, interviews with ZAV partners
2. German Language Training (to B1) 6–12 months Fully funded by the program. Can be done in parallel with other steps.
3. Job Matching & Interviews 2–4 months ZAV arranges interviews with German hospitals and care facilities
4. Visa & Pre-Departure 1–2 months Work visa processing, health checks, orientation meetings
5. Professional Recognition (Anerkennung) 6–12 months Completed after arrival in Germany, with employer and GIZ support

Practical tip: Steps 2 and 3 can overlap, saving you several months overall.

How It Works

The G2G Process: Step by Step

The Triple Win program is straightforward because it's government-managed. Here's what to expect:

  1. Register with BP2MI: Submit your application through the official BP2MI portal (SISKOP2MI) during open recruitment rounds, typically twice per year.
  2. Language Training: Once accepted, you begin German language classes (A1 to B1) at no cost. Training centers are available in Bandung, Surabaya, and online.
  3. Professional Preparation: GIZ provides orientation on the German healthcare system, professional standards, and cultural integration.
  4. Job Matching: ZAV matches you with German hospitals or elderly care facilities based on your preferences and qualifications.
  5. Visa & Deployment: After job interviews, you receive a formal job offer and work visa. GIZ assists with all pre-departure logistics.
  6. Integration & Recognition: Upon arrival, you work as an assistant nurse while completing your professional recognition. GIZ provides an integration advisor throughout your first year.

Life in Germany

Your First Year: Recognition and Salary

Upon arrival in Germany, you will work as an assistant nurse (Pflegehelfer) while completing your professional recognition (Anerkennung). Most Indonesian nurses complete this process within 12 months.

  • Initial Salary: As an assistant nurse, you can expect an initial salary of approximately IDR 25–30 million per month.
  • Recognized Nurse Salary: Once your qualifications are fully recognized, salaries typically range from €2,800 to €3,500 per month, consistent with standard German nursing pay scales.
  • Integration Support: GIZ provides a dedicated integration advisor to help with administrative tasks, housing, language refinement, and settling in during your first year.
  • Family Reunification: After establishing yourself, you have the legal right to bring your family to Germany.
Your Educational Foundation

Where You Trained Matters Too

Your professional credibility in Germany depends not just on your experience, but also on the reputation of your nursing school. German employers and recognition authorities review your educational background carefully. Graduates from well-established nursing programs have a stronger profile during the Anerkennung process.

How Berlin Gateway Supports You

Where Berlin Gateway Fits In

To be clear: Berlin Gateway is not part of the Triple Win program, and we do not replace it. If you qualify for Triple Win, apply directly—it's free and government-run. Where we add real value is in the gaps the program doesn't cover:

  • Missed an application round? BP2MI opens applications only twice a year. If you're between rounds, we help you explore direct recruitment by German hospitals outside the G2G window.
  • State-specific Anerkennung guidance. Recognition requirements vary by German state (Bundesland). We help you understand exactly what your specific state will require before you commit to a placement.
  • Beyond the integration advisor. GIZ's integration advisor covers the basics. We help with the practical specifics—housing search, understanding your contract terms, and planning for family reunification timing.
