Why Germany?

Considering the Gulf? Tunisia Has Its Own Official Triple Win Program.

If you're a Tunisian nurse weighing your options abroad, you've likely looked at the Gulf. But Tunisia holds a distinct position among Arab countries: it is one of only four countries worldwide with a formal, government-to-government partnership in Germany's Triple Win programme — the same free, officially managed pathway used by nurses from the Philippines and Indonesia. Morocco and Algeria do not currently have this same formal arrangement; Tunisia does.

Tunisia's Triple Win partnership began in 2018, coordinated on the Tunisian side by ANETI (Agence Nationale pour l'Emploi et le Travail Indépendant — the National Agency for Employment and Self-Employment), working directly with Germany's Federal Employment Agency (ZAV) and supported on the ground by GIZ, which maintains its own office and local team in Tunis.

How It Works

How Tunisia's Triple Win Process Works

  1. ANETI publishes a call for applicants on its website, along with all required documents and eligibility conditions.
  2. Applications are submitted directly to ANETI, which pre-screens candidates before forwarding qualified profiles.
  3. ZAV conducts selection interviews inside Tunisia — evaluating training, professional experience, motivation, and (where relevant) existing German language ability.
  4. Accepted candidates begin German language training in Tunisia, funded through the programme, working toward the mandatory B1 certificate required before departure.
  5. GIZ's local team supports professional preparation — orientation on German workplace culture, the healthcare system, and what to expect on arrival.
  6. Placement and departure — GIZ coordinates visa logistics and connects you with a specific German employer (hospital or elderly care facility) before you leave Tunisia.
Beyond Tunis

German Language Preparation — Not Just in the Capital

Goethe-Institut Tunis (6 Rue du Sénégal, Tunis-Belvédère) is the main Goethe exam center in the country, offering courses from A1 and TestDaF exams twice yearly. But if you're outside Tunis, you don't need to relocate:

Sousse — Goethe-Institut, Getusion, INTO Academy Sfax — Getusion (Certified exam center)

TELC and other internationally recognized certificates issued through these centers carry the same weight as Goethe certificates for Anerkennung purposes.

Arrival & Integration

What Happens After You Arrive

Nurses placed through Triple Win typically begin work while completing the German recognition process (Anerkennung), with GIZ's integration advisor providing ongoing support through the first year — administrative steps, housing, and settling in. Starting salary for recognized nurses generally falls between €2,800 and €3,500 per month, consistent with standard German nursing pay scales, alongside full health and social insurance coverage.

Our Role

Where This Fits With Berlin Gateway

Triple Win is a government-to-government programme — ANETI and GIZ manage the placement and preparation pipeline directly. To be clear: Berlin Gateway is not part of the Triple Win programme, and we do not replace it. If you qualify, apply directly through ANETI — it's free and government-run. Where we add real value is in the gaps the programme doesn't cover:

  • Missed an application round? ANETI opens applications only a few times a year. Between rounds, we help you explore direct recruitment by German hospitals outside the G2G window.
  • State-specific Anerkennung guidance. Recognition requirements vary by German state (Bundesland). We help you understand exactly what your specific state will require before you commit to a placement.
  • Beyond the integration advisor. GIZ's integration advisor covers the basics. We help with the practical specifics — housing search, understanding your contract terms, and planning for family reunification timing.
Conclusion

Bottom Line

Tunisia's position is genuinely different from its Maghreb neighbors: a real, tested, government-backed corridor to Germany has existed since 2018, with thousands of nurses already placed across all Triple Win countries combined. If you're comparing the Gulf to Germany, this structural advantage — a vetted, free, state-to-state pathway — is worth understanding before you commit to either direction.
