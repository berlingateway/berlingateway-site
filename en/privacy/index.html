2. General information on data processing

As a rule, we process our users’ personal data only to the extent necessary to provide a functional website as well as our content and services. Personal data are generally processed only with the user’s consent or on another legal basis pursuant to Article 6(1) GDPR.

3. Your rights as a data subject

Within the scope of applicable legal provisions, you have the right at any time to:

  • Access your personal data stored by us (Article 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate personal data (Article 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure of your data stored by us (Article 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing, where legal requirements are met (Article 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability, where you have consented to data processing or concluded a contract with us (Article 20 GDPR)
  • Object to our processing of your data (Article 21 GDPR)
  • Withdraw consent previously given with effect for the future, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority concerning our processing of your personal data. The competent authority is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Friedrichstr. 219, 10969 Berlin.

4. Hosting and provision of the website

This website is delivered through Cloudflare. When the website is accessed, technical information is collected automatically and stored for a limited period in so-called server log files, including:

  • IP address (where applicable, shortened/anonymised)
  • Date and time of the request
  • Page / file accessed
  • Referrer URL
  • Browser and operating system used

These data are used to ensure the smooth operation of the website and to evaluate system security and stability (legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest).

5. Analytics tools

We use the analytics service Umami for anonymised/pseudonymised analysis of user behaviour on our website (for example, pages accessed, time spent, and visitor origin). No cookies are set and no unique allocation to an identifiable person is made. Processing is based on our legitimate interest in designing our website in line with needs (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We use no other analytics, advertising or remarketing services (in particular, no Google Analytics, no Google/Meta advertising pixels, and no LinkedIn Insight Tag).

6. Contacting us

6.1 Contact form / email / telephone

If you contact us by email, telephone or via a contact form, your information (including your name, contact details and the content of your enquiry) is stored by us for the purpose of processing your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to enquiries).

Your data are deleted as soon as the purpose of storage no longer applies, i.e. as soon as your enquiry has been conclusively processed, unless statutory retention obligations prevent this.

6.2 WhatsApp

Our website offers the possibility of contacting us via WhatsApp. WhatsApp is a service of WhatsApp Ireland Limited, 4 Grand Canal Square, Grand Canal Harbour, Dublin 2, Ireland. When you click a WhatsApp link, a connection to WhatsApp is established; the processing of your data within the WhatsApp app is subject to the privacy policy of WhatsApp/Meta. We have no influence on this processing. Further information can be found in WhatsApp’s privacy policy.

6.3 Forms (Tally)

For certain enquiries (for example, application/assessment forms), we use the form service Tally (Tally, available at tally.so). The data you enter in the form (name, contact details, professional qualifications, work experience and language skills) are processed through Tally’s infrastructure and transmitted to us. These forms do not ask for health data or other special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR.

The legal basis for processing is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures or contract initiation).

7. Processing of applicant/candidate data

In the context of our placement activities, we process personal data of applicants and candidates who apply via our website or directly to us or submit a profile, including:

  • Contact details (name, email, telephone)
  • Professional qualifications and evidence of vocational training and studies
  • Work experience
  • Language skills

The purpose of processing is placement with interested employers and support in the recognition and integration process. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures or performance of a contract).

Disclosure to employers: Candidate data are disclosed to interested employers only with your prior knowledge and within the scope of the placement process.

Your data are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed for the purpose of placement, unless statutory retention obligations prevent this.

8. Processing employer/company data (B2B)

When companies contact us through our employer pages (for example, under “Unternehmen”), we process the contact and needs data provided solely to handle the enquiry and, where applicable, to prepare a collaboration (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

9. Data security

Within the scope of applicable legal provisions, we take appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure the security of your data and to protect them against loss, misuse or unauthorised access. Data are transmitted through this website in encrypted form via HTTPS.
