How much does an Egyptian nurse earn in Germany?

Official pay tables and legal minimum wages, with sources.

P6 · Assistant, 1+ year of training
€3,012.49
per month (gross), full-time
First step. Assistant with at least one year of training and matching duties
P7 · Nurse with German professional recognition
€3,510.30
per month (gross), full-time
Step 2 (P7 has no step 1). Nurse with German professional recognition, in a matching position

Municipal hospitals covered by TVöD-K (pay table P, valid from 1 May 2026): full-time is 38.5 hours per week (39 in Baden-Württemberg). P5, P6 and P7 are different roles, not automatic promotions for the same person.

Care homes and other eligible care providers (statutory minimum wage per hour, from 1 July 2026): assistants €16.52, staff with at least one year of training €17.80, qualified nurses €21.03. From 1 July 2027: €16.95, €18.26 and €21.58. Hospitals are not covered by this regulation, and it does not apply to trainees. Many employers pay more under collective agreements. Monthly pay depends on contracted hours.

During recognition: pay during recognition depends on the duties you are employed to perform and your contract; recognition does not itself determine a P5, P6 or P7 salary. Trainee pay is set separately. Net pay depends on your personal situation.

Sources: VKA pay table P (Anlage E to TVöD-K), valid from 1 May 2026; § 6 TVöD-K; Siebte Pflegearbeitsbedingungenverordnung (Bundesanzeiger, BAnz AT 15.01.2026 B1).

Your pathway to Germany

Your route depends on your qualification

In Egypt it's called vocational nursing training (تدريب مهني). Whether and how it is recognized in Germany is decided case by case by the responsible authority.

Pathway 1

Vocational Nursing Diploma

  • ✓ 3-year nursing diploma from a health institute
  • ✓ Recognition is decided case by case
  • ✓ May require a bridging course or a knowledge exam
  • ✓ German language B2 is required for the nursing license
  • ✓ Recognition and a professional license are required before working as a nurse
Pathway 2

Bachelor's in Nursing

  • ✓ Assessed individually by the recognition authority
  • ✓ Greater opportunities for specialization
  • ✓ Pay depends on the role and the employer's pay scale
  • ✓ Can open access to supervisory and leadership roles
  • ✓ German language B2 is required for the nursing license
